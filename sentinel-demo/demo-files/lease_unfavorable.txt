THIS LEASE is made on the date stated in the Prescribed Clauses BETWEEN the Landlord and the Tenant.

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION
1.1 Definitions
"Annual Rent": £210,000 per annum, subject to Upwards Only Rent Review.
"Building": The entire building of which the Property forms part, including the roof, foundations, external walls, and all structural parts.
"Interest Rate": 5% above the base rate of the Landlord's choosing.
"Service Charge": The Tenant’s proportion (as conclusively determined by the Landlord’s Surveyor whose decision shall be final and binding) of the Service Costs.
"Service Costs": All costs, expenses, fees, and outgoings of whatsoever nature incurred by the Landlord in relation to the Building, including but not limited to repair, renewal, replacement, improvement, and decoration of the structure, roof, and foundations.

1.2 Interpretation
References to "consent" mean consent in writing signed by the Landlord, which may be granted or withheld at the Landlord's absolute discretion unless otherwise stated.

2. DEMISE AND RENT
2.1 The Landlord demises the Property to the Tenant for the Term YIELDING AND PAYING:
(a) The Annual Rent;
(b) The Service Charge;
(c) The Insurance Rent;
(d) All other sums due under this Lease.
2.2 Rent shall be paid by Standing Order on the Quarter Days. Acceptance of rent by the Landlord shall not waive any breach.

3. TENANT’S COVENANTS
3.1 Payment
The Tenant shall pay the Annual Rent, Service Charge, and Insurance Rent on the Quarter Days without any deduction, set-off, equitable set-off, or counterclaim whatsoever.

3.2 Repair ("Put and Keep")
The Tenant shall at all times keep the Property (including all conducting media, landlord’s fixtures, and the structural parts of the Property where comprised in the Demise) in good and substantial repair and condition, regardless of the state of repair at the date of this Lease. The Tenant acknowledges that the Property may not be in such condition at the start of the Term and accepts the obligation to put the Property into such condition.

3.3 Indemnity
The Tenant shall indemnify and keep the Landlord fully indemnified against all losses, claims, demands, actions, proceedings, damages, costs (including legal costs on a full indemnity basis), expenses, or other liability arising in any way from this Lease, any breach of the Tenant's covenants, or any act or omission of the Tenant.

3.4 Alterations
The Tenant shall not make any alterations or additions to the Property (whether structural or non-structural, internal or external) without the Landlord's prior written consent, which may be withheld at the Landlord’s absolute discretion.

3.5 Alienation
(a) Not to assign, underlet, or part with possession of the whole or any part of the Property without the Landlord's consent.
(b) The Landlord may withhold consent to an assignment if, in the Landlord's opinion, the proposed assignee is of lower financial standing than the Tenant was at the date of this Lease or at the date of assignment (whichever is higher).
(c) Upon any assignment, the Tenant shall provide an Authorised Guarantee Agreement (AGA).

3.6 Costs
To pay on an indemnity basis all costs and expenses incurred by the Landlord in connection with the preparation and service of a schedule of dilapidations or any notice under section 146 of the Law of Property Act 1925.

4. LANDLORD’S COVENANTS
4.1 Quiet Enjoyment
Subject to the Tenant paying the Rent and performing the covenants, the Tenant may quietly enjoy the Property.

4.2 Exclusion of Liability
The Landlord shall not be liable to the Tenant for any failure to provide Services caused by mechanical breakdown, labour disputes, shortage of fuel or materials, or any other cause beyond the Landlord's control.

5. SERVICE CHARGE AND ADMINISTRATION
5.1 Scope
The Tenant shall be responsible for its proportion of the cost of repairs, maintenance, and replacement of the structural parts of the Building, including the roof and foundations, notwithstanding that such works may constitute improvements or capital expenditure.

5.2 Administration Fee
The Tenant shall pay an administration and management fee equal to 15% of the total Service Costs incurred in each year. This fee is fixed and not subject to challenge or cap.

5.3 Sinking Fund
The Landlord may collect a sinking fund for future expenditure, the amount of which shall be determined by the Landlord in its absolute discretion.

6. RENT REVIEW
6.1 Upwards Only
The Rent shall be reviewed on the 5th anniversary to the higher of (a) the passing rent or (b) the Open Market Rent.

6.2 Assumptions (The "Hypothetical Lease")
The Open Market Rent shall be calculated on the assumption that:
(a) The Tenant has fully complied with all repairing obligations (even if they have not).
(b) The Property is fit for immediate occupation and use.
(c) The Property has been fully fitted out by the Landlord ready for the Tenant's occupation.
(d) All Tenant's improvements are to be included in the valuation (i.e., the Tenant pays rent on their own improvements).

7. BREAK CLAUSE (LANDLORD ONLY)
7.1 Landlord’s Break
The Landlord may terminate this Lease on the fifth anniversary of the Term Commencement Date by serving not less than three (3) months' written notice on the Tenant.
7.2 Tenant’s Break
[Intentionally Omitted] Note: No right for Tenant to break.

8. FORFEITURE
8.1 The Landlord may re-enter the Property and forfeit the Lease if:
(a) Any Rent remains unpaid for 7 days after becoming due; or
(b) The Tenant breaches any covenant; or
(c) The Tenant enters into any arrangement with creditors or suffers any distress or execution to be levied on the Property.
